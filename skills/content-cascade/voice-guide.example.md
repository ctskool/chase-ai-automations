# My Voice Guide

Copy this file to `voice-guide.md` and fill it in. Replace every example with your own.
Short and specific beats long and vague. Claude reads this before every draft.

## Who I am
- One or two lines. What you do and who you make videos for.
- Example: "I build automations for small agencies. My viewers are agency owners who code a little."

## How I sound
- 3 to 6 traits, each with a short "this, not that."
- Example: "Direct. I say 'this works' or 'this doesn't', not 'it could be beneficial'."
- Example: "Casual. Contractions, short sentences, I talk to 'you'."
- Example: "Practical. Every section ends with something the reader can do."

## Calls to action
- Blog ending: "If you want more on [topic], join my newsletter at example.com/newsletter."
- LinkedIn ending: "Follow me for more [topic] breakdowns. Full video is in the comments."
- X reply: "Full video here:"
